# Arena art

`stone.webp` and `canopy.webp` were generated for this project with OpenAI image generation on 2026-10-26, then resized and compressed to WebP for delivery. These are original generated fantasy textures, not extracted League of Legends assets. The generated PNG originals remain in the creation workspace.

Stone: flat seamless worn slate pavement, subtle moss, muted cool colors. Canopy: isolated overhead oak crown, transparent background, painterly leaves. Delivery sizes: 1024×1024 and 768×768. Canopies use alpha testing and instancing; textures are cached and cloned per arena ownership.

KayKit models and their CC0 licenses remain in `../kaykit`; no Riot character, map or VFX asset is distributed here.
